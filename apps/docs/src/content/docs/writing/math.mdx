MDLeaf renders LaTeX math with [KaTeX](https://katex.org/).

- **Inline math**: `$E = mc^2$`. The opening `$` must not be followed by a space and the closing `$` must not be followed by a digit, so prices such as `$5 and $10` stay text.
- **Display math**: `$$` on its own line, the formula, and `$$` on its own line. A one-line `$$x^2$$` also works.

## Limits

KaTeX supports most of LaTeX math mode but not every package. Invalid math is shown as red error text and reported in the problems panel as `math-error` with KaTeX's message. Commands that would load external resources (`\href`, `\url`, `\includegraphics`) are not allowed.

Display formulas are never split across pages. If one is taller than a page, MDLeaf warns about it.
