Write a footnote reference with `[^label]` and define it anywhere on its own line with `[^label]: text`. Labels can be numbers or words.

```md
Trees exchange water through their roots.[^1]

[^1]: Observed in beech forests.
```

**Insert → Footnote** (<Shortcut command="insert.footnote" />) inserts the next free number and adds the definition at the end of the document for you.

## Endnotes, not page footnotes

MDLeaf renders footnotes as **endnotes**: a numbered list after a rule at the end of the document, with links back to the reference. They flow over pages like normal text. If the document has a table of contents, the notes section is not listed in it.
