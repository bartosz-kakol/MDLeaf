## Create a document

In the Library choose **+ New document**, give it a title and pick a profile (the page size and theme). You land in the editor.

The editor screen has the Markdown source on the left and the preview on the right. Your work is saved automatically half a second after you stop typing; the top bar shows _Saved_, _Saving…_ or _Unsaved changes_.

## Draft and page view

The preview has two modes. Toggle them with the **Draft | Page** buttons or <Shortcut command="view.toggleMode" />.

- **Draft view** shows one continuous column with the width of your page's text area, so lines wrap exactly as on the page. Page breaks and other directives appear as small markers.
- **Page view** lays the document out on real pages, with running headers and footers, page numbers and the table of contents filled in. It updates shortly after you stop typing.

The preview follows the editor as you scroll. Turn this off with the scroll-follow button in the preview's toolbar.

## Layouts

Use **Editor | Split | Preview** (<Shortcut command="view.cycleLayout" /> cycles them) to focus on writing or reading.

## Export a PDF

Choose **Export PDF** (<Shortcut command="file.exportPdf" />). MDLeaf prepares the pages and opens the browser's print dialog.

1. Set the destination to **Save as PDF**.
2. Set **Margins** to **None** or **Default** — the pages already contain their margins.
3. Turn off the browser's **Headers and footers** option.

The PDF contains real, selectable text. Links stay clickable; links inside the document, like table-of-contents entries, are clickable in Chromium-based browsers and best-effort in Firefox and Safari.

If the document has problems that may affect the PDF — missing images or blocks taller than a page — MDLeaf tells you before printing.
