```md
::pagebreak
```

Starts a new page in page view and in the PDF. In draft view it shows as a dashed line labelled *Page break*. It takes no label and no attributes. Insert one with <Shortcut command="insert.pageBreak" />.

<LiveExample name="page-breaks" />

## Rules

- **Consecutive breaks collapse.** Two `::pagebreak` lines in a row produce one break, not an empty page. A break at the very start or end of the document does nothing. A break that falls exactly where a page ends anyway does not add a page either.
- **Blank pages on purpose**: put a paragraph containing only a non-breaking space between two breaks:

  ```md
  ::pagebreak

  &nbsp;

  ::pagebreak
  ```

- Directives that apply to a page, such as `::hide` or `::pagenumber`, belong right after the break: then they are guaranteed to land on the new page.

## Common mistakes

- `  ::pagebreak` (indented) or `- ::pagebreak` (in a list) is text: `directive-misplaced`.
- `::page-break` is not a directive: `directive-unknown`.
- `::pagebreak{now}`: attributes are ignored with `directive-attr-unknown`.
